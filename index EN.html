Our Legal Specialties

Practice Areas and Litigation in Medellín and Colombia

We combine more than 14 years of experience in judicial forums with investigative rigor for drafting contracts and providing procedural representation for individuals, families, and companies.

Family Law & International Child Abduction

We protect the financial stability and well-being of your family through contentious representation and highly rigorous legal solutions at the national and international level.

  • ✓ Mutual-agreement divorces before a notary and contentious divorces before Family Courts.
  • ✓ Judicial and notarial liquidation of marital property and de facto marital union assets.
  • ✓ Judicial representation in establishing, increasing, terminating, and enforcing child support (executive process for minors).
  • ✓ Regulation of custody, physical care, and visitation arrangements.
  • ✓ Litigation in International Child Return (1980 Hague Convention) and minor exit permits.
  • ✓ Probate proceedings and notarial estate settlements.
  • ✓ Wills.

Civil Law, Contracts & Real Estate

We draft and review contracts with comprehensive legal protection and represent your interests in civil litigation before the ordinary jurisdiction to safeguard your assets.

  • ✓ Drafting, review, and legal audit of contracts, settlements, promise-of-sale agreements, public deeds, and real estate contracts.
  • ✓ Contentious civil litigation: Judicial representation in breach of contract and tort liability claims for damages.
  • ✓ In-depth study of the chain of title and contractual risk analysis.
  • ✓ Drafting, structuring, and negotiation of civil contracts (lease, exchange, loan for use, usufruct, loan for consumption, and guarantees).
  • ✓ Representation in adverse possession lawsuits (ordinary and extraordinary acquisitive prescription - art. 375 of the CGP).
  • ✓ Executive litigation and judicial collection of negotiable instruments (promissory notes, bills of exchange, checks, and invoices).

Corporate Law & Shareholder Litigation

We support entrepreneurs in drafting commercial contracts, corporate governance, and procedural representation in commercial and shareholder disputes in Colombia.

  • ✓ Ongoing corporate counsel: labor, commercial, and contractual law.
  • ✓ Drafting, review, and negotiation of commercial contracts (supply, distribution, commercial agency, commercial lease, and provision of services).
  • ✓ Incorporation of companies (SAS - Law 1258 of 2008), bylaw amendments, and shareholder agreements.
  • ✓ Shareholder and corporate litigation, and dispute resolution between partners before the Superintendency of Companies (Mercantile Procedures Delegation) and Circuit Judges.
  • ✓ Corporate governance, family business protocols, and commercial contract auditing.
  • ✓ Business reorganization and insolvency proceedings (Law 1116 of 2006).

Criminal Law & Trial Defense

Representation and technical defense in judicial forums within the Accusatory Criminal System (Law 906 of 2004), safeguarding liberty and the protection of victims and defendants.

  • ✓ Technical criminal defense for natural persons and judicial representation of crime victims in criminal proceedings.
  • ✓ Economic and corporate crime (fraud, breach of trust, procedural fraud, financial offenses, and anti-corruption).
  • ✓ Representation in preliminary hearings (legalization of arrest, imputation, and pretrial detention measures), preparatory hearing, and oral trial.
  • ✓ Filing of well-structured criminal complaints and denunciations before the Attorney General's Office.

Mining & Environmental Law and Title Management

We support small and medium-sized miners, companies, and investors in the comprehensive legal management of mining titles, formalization, contracting, and procedural representation before mining and environmental authorities.

  • ✓ Management, due diligence, and permanent monitoring of mining titles and applications before the National Mining Agency (ANM).
  • ✓ Drafting, viability assessment, and monitoring of Mining Concession Contract proposals and contracts with differential requirements (Law 2250 of 2022).
  • ✓ Mining formalization and legalization processes: Formalization subcontracts, Special Reserve Areas (ARE), area relinquishment, and Temporary Environmental License procedures.
  • ✓ Structuring, precontractual viability analysis, and execution of Mining Operating Contracts and Rights Assignment Contracts (Law 685 of 2001).
  • ✓ Counsel on establishing and judicially imposing Mining Easements (Law 1274 of 2009), damages claims, and administrative protection against interference.
  • ✓ Appraisal and claims for comprehensive reparation of patrimonial damages arising from mining activity and its contracts.
  • ✓ Environmental management and procedures: Environmental diagnosis, Environmental Licenses, Environmental Impact Studies (EIA), Environmental Management Plans (PMA), and Environmental Compliance Reports (ICA).

Let's Answer Your Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Practical information on attention, coverage, timelines, fees, and specialized areas.

Can I pursue a legal process in Colombia if I live abroad or I am a foreigner?▼
Yes, 100% remote. We represent foreign citizens and Colombian nationals residing abroad (USA, Europe, Latin America) with no need for them to travel to Colombia. Through special powers of attorney granted before consulates or apostilled, and applying the digital justice rules (Law 2213 of 2022), we handle notarial procedures, virtual hearings, and judicial litigation throughout Colombian territory.
How does the initial legal consultation work?▼
We offer in-person consultations at our main office located in El Poblado (Calle 10 #42-45, Medellín) and virtual consultations via video call for clients in any city of Colombia or abroad. In this analytical session we review the prior documentation and map out the strategic roadmap for the case.
In which cities and regions do you represent judicial cases?▼
We take on judicial representation throughout Colombia. Our main office is in Medellín (with a constant presence in the Aburrá Valley and Eastern Antioquia), and through digital litigation and remote powers of attorney we operate directly before courts and tribunals across the country.
How are International Child Abduction cases handled under the Hague Convention?▼
The wrongful removal or retention of a child demands immediate action. We apply the 1980 Hague Convention in coordination with the Colombian Institute of Family Welfare (ICBF) and Family Courts to seek the safe return of the child to his or her country of habitual residence, rigorously analyzing the legal exceptions provided in the Convention itself (such as grave risk, integration, or the child's reasoned objection).
How long does it take to resolve a legal process in Colombia?▼
Timelines vary according to the legal route and instance: notarial procedures by mutual agreement (divorces or estate settlements) are usually resolved within an average of 15 to 45 business days, depending on the workload and response times of the assigned notary office. Contentious litigation before Judges of the Republic depends on the court's docket, the taking of evidence, and the development of hearings under the General Code of Procedure.
What legal safeguards do you offer for property purchases and real estate investment?▼
We carry out thorough studies of the chain of title (20 years), verification of liens, encumbrances, or limitations on ownership, and we draft or audit the promise of sale. We protect the capital of buyers and investors before they make any disbursement or sign any public deed.
How are fees structured and what payment facilities do you offer?▼
We operate under a Transparent Fee Policy with No Hidden Costs. We provide written quotes with fixed fees or a contingency fee basis (percentage over results in cases where legally applicable). We accept local bank transfers and international wires for clients abroad.
What assurance do I have that my case is viable before filing a lawsuit?▼
In the Initial Strategic Diagnosis we evaluate the background and applicable case law. If your case lacks legal viability or the economic risks outweigh the benefits, we will tell you with absolute ethical honesty before you incur any judicial or contractual expenses.
Do you provide ongoing legal counsel to companies in Colombia?▼
Yes. We offer ongoing counsel to companies in Colombia on labor, contractual, corporate, and business matters. We accompany daily regulatory compliance, drafting of commercial contracts, structuring of shareholder agreements, and representation in litigation before the ordinary jurisdiction and the Superintendency of Companies.
